# Worked Example: Business Project and Outstanding Payment

Summary: a verbal agreement for RMB 30,000, with RMB 6,000 paid to start. The user has delivered part of the work, but the client no longer wants the remaining service and may resist further payment.

## User input

```text
[Negotiation type]
Outstanding payment / business partnership

[Background]
We verbally agreed on RMB 30,000 for Xiaohongshu content services: account positioning, topics, scripts, and four coaching sessions. The client paid RMB 6,000 to start. I completed the account assessment, topic plan, and two sessions. They now say they no longer need the service, and may not want to pay the balance.

[My role and authority]
Service provider and direct implementer.

[Other party]
The client company's owner, with final authority.

[Objective]
At least collect payment for completed work; ideally continue settlement against the RMB 30,000 agreement.

[Walk-away limit]
If they cancel, completed work should be paid up to RMB 18,000. RMB 6,000 alone is not acceptable.

[BATNA]
Preserve chats, files, and meeting records; negotiate first, then consult a lawyer or assess formal collection / platform complaint processes.

[Their possible interests]
Reduce or avoid further payment because they no longer need the remaining service.

[My bargaining assets]
WeChat records, transfer records, deliverables, meeting recording summaries, and acknowledgment of some proposals.

[My weakness]
No formal contract, only verbal agreement and WeChat records.

[Evidence]
WeChat chats, RMB 6,000 transfer, documents, two meeting records.

[Channel]
WeChat, phone if needed.

[Style]
Measured / preserve goodwill / clear boundaries.

[Output]
Strategy / three options / WeChat wording / risk review / confirmation.
```

## Example output

### 1. Situation

Primary scenario: business cooperation and outstanding payment. Separate cancellation of future service from settlement for value already delivered.

Stage: partial delivery, with possible early termination.

Priority risks:

- No formal contract; rely on chats, payment, and delivery evidence.
- The client may conflate no longer needing future service with not paying for completed work.
- Emotional demands could reduce room for settlement.

Use measured boundaries: confirm facts and completed services, then discuss continued performance or stage settlement.

### 2. Power index

| Dimension | User | Other party |
|---|---:|---:|
| Alternatives | 5 | 6 |
| Time pressure | 4 | 5 |
| Information | 7 | 5 |
| Resource control | 5 | 6 |
| Evidence | 6 | 4 |
| Relationship dependence | 4 | 4 |
| Total | 31/60 | 30/60 |

Roughly balanced. Evidence is imperfect but delivery records exist. Start with facts and stage settlement rather than hard pressure.

### 3. Information gaps

Required:

- Is RMB 30,000 explicitly recorded in WeChat?
- Are four sessions and scope confirmed in writing?
- What were the client's exact words about cancellation?

Helpful:

- Proportion of the original package delivered.
- Acknowledgment of the assessment and topic plan.

Provisional assumptions:

- RMB 6,000 was a starting payment, not the full fee.
- Completed work is worth more than RMB 6,000.

### 4. Goals

- Ideal: continue the original RMB 30,000 engagement, deliver the remainder, and settle.
- Acceptable: cancel remaining services and bring payment for completed work to RMB 18,000.
- Limit: RMB 6,000 cannot cover all completed assessment, planning, and coaching.
- External wording: "Completed work needs separate settlement" rather than "My minimum is RMB 18,000."

### 5. BATNA / ZOPA

- User BATNA: preserve evidence, continue discussions, and consult a lawyer on collection or dispute processes if necessary.
- Other party's possible BATNA: stop services and try to pay only the starting amount.
- Possible ZOPA:
  - Continue the RMB 30,000 engagement with clear remaining scope and payments.
  - Cancel and settle completed work at a total of RMB 15,000–20,000.
  - Close at RMB 12,000–15,000 with use of delivered materials and no further coaching.

Probe: "Do you no longer need future services, or do you have specific objections to the completed work?"

### 6. Bargaining map

User assets:

- WeChat agreement, transfer, delivery, and meeting records.
- Completed assessment, topic plan, and two sessions.
- Future service, material permissions, closeout explanation.

Other party's assets:

- Unpaid balance.
- Ambiguity from the lack of a formal contract.

Variables:

- Continuation or cancellation.
- Settlement amount for completed work.
- Material usage rights.
- Further question-and-answer sessions.
- Payment timing.

### 7. Other party's profile

- Stated request: no further service.
- Possible interests: reduce spending and further investment.
- Possible pressures: changed priorities, tighter budget, reduced importance of the content project.
- Authority: high; the owner decides.
- Likely next move: "You have not finished, so no balance is due."

### 8. Strategy

1. Establish the distinction between completed work and future cancellation before arguing breach.
2. Allow cancellation while requesting settlement for completed value.
3. Present three options to shift the discussion from whether to pay to how to settle.
4. Keep WeChat records and confirm calls in writing.

Avoid threats of exposure or litigation, accusations of evading payment, and further free service in exchange for uncertain payment.

### 9. Offers

**A: continue the original engagement**

- User receives: continuation and settlement at RMB 30,000.
- Client receives: all four sessions and remaining delivery.
- Concession: revised scheduling.
- Return: confirmed remaining payment milestones and schedule.
- Risk: weak commitment may lead to further delay.
- Fits: temporary hesitation.

**B: stage settlement and closeout**

- User receives: total payment brought to RMB 18,000.
- Client receives: cancellation of future work and usage rights for delivered materials.
- Concession: no requirement to continue the full RMB 30,000 engagement.
- Return: payment by a specified date and confirmation that this stage is settled.
- Risk: amount remains negotiable.
- Fits: a clear desire to stop.

**C: low-conflict compromise**

- User receives: a further RMB 9,000–12,000 and an amicable close.
- Client receives: one closing call and organized materials.
- Concession: below the ideal settlement for completed work.
- Return: no further revisions or coaching.
- Risk: lower user return.
- Fits: prioritizing quick loss limitation and the relationship.

### 10. Scores

| Option | Value | Agreement likelihood | Risk control | Relationship | Execution clarity | Total | Rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| A: continue | 5 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 16 | 3 |
| B: stage settlement | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 21 | 1 |
| C: compromise | 3 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 21 | 2 |

Lead with B: it fits a client who wants to stop while recovering value already delivered.

### 11. First message

```text
[name], I understand you may no longer need the remaining service. We can respect that and separately confirm how to settle completed work.

I have completed the account assessment, topic plan, and first two coaching sessions. The RMB 6,000 was a starting payment and does not cover all completed services.

If you want to continue, let's confirm remaining sessions and payment milestones under the original engagement. If you prefer to stop, let's agree a stage settlement, material usage rights, and the boundary on further services.

Which direction should we clarify today?
```

### 12. Reactions and replies

"You have not finished; why should I pay?"

```text
The remaining services have not been delivered, so I am not treating everything as complete. I am asking to settle the completed portion separately. Cancellation of future work does not make the assessment, plan, and two sessions all part of the RMB 6,000 starting payment.
```

"We never signed a contract."

```text
There is no formal contract, so I would like to work from our WeChat records, payments, and actual delivery to find a fair closeout for the agreed and completed services.
```

"I can only pay a little more."

```text
We can discuss an amount alongside what it covers. For example, total payment of [amount] could include use of completed materials and one closing explanation, with no further coaching or revisions.
```

### 13. Risk review

Risk: medium.

- Avoid categorical legal characterizations without a formal contract.
- Do not write "You breached the contract" or "I will definitely sue."
- Keep the RMB 18,000 limit private.
- Prefer "stage settlement for completed work" to "you must pay the balance."
- Invite corrections to the written understanding.

### 14. Confirmation

```text
To confirm today's discussion:
1. You may not continue the full service.
2. I completed the assessment, topic plan, and two sessions.
3. We need to confirm the settlement amount, payment date, and material usage rights.
4. If continuing, we will confirm the remaining schedule and payments; otherwise, close through stage settlement.

Please flag any misunderstanding today. Otherwise, let's use these points to agree the amount and timing.
```

### 15. Next actions

- Send the first message without quoting the limit.
- Record whether the client acknowledges completed services.
- If payment is refused, organize evidence and consult a lawyer or qualified adviser.
- If settlement is accepted, document amount, date, usage rights, and service boundaries immediately.
