## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-01

### Added

- Initial `negotiation-war-room-skill` release.
- Core protocol, now named `SKILL.md` in this fork.
- System, input, output, simulation, risk review, and debrief prompts.
- BATNA, ZOPA, anchoring, interest-based negotiation, MESO, trade-offs, loss aversion, and commitments.
- Power index, scenario routing, concession rules, MESO offer rules, and risk checklist.
- Eight playbooks: payments, discounts, compensation, contracts, partners, disputes, procurement and sales, relationships.
- WeChat, email, phone, meeting, minutes, and follow-up templates.
- Full payment, discount, compensation, and partnership examples.
- Test prompts, edge cases, and acceptance checklist.
- GitHub issue and PR templates, contribution guidance, safety policy, and conduct rules.

### Security

- Prohibited deception, threats, extortion, fabricated evidence, unlawful pressure, lawyer impersonation, and malicious manipulation.
- No guaranteed negotiation success.
- Legal, employment arbitration, and contract disputes limited to communication strategy, evidence organization, and risk considerations.
